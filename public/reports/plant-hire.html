Overview

Gross Profit = sum of the Profit column in the month's Day Book sheet (Invoice Value − Expenses − Workshop Cost) · Net Profit = Gross Profit − Overheads. This matches the workbook's own Net Profit cells (e.g. June's sheet states net profit of QAR 170.96, which is exactly Gross Profit − Overheads for June). Headline totals are taken from each sheet's own "Totals:" row.
